# Web copy — make the value legible

Use this pack for marketing, adoption, landing and homepage copy, including a developer
tool's website. It is not the grammar for operating a product, a reference manual or a
recovery message. Those surfaces may need an exact mechanism or instruction first.
This pack supplies writing decisions, not a new pipeline or a universal page template.
The existing writer, copy deck, copy editor and invalidation rules remain authoritative.

## Start with the reader's decision

A factual inventory is not yet a persuasive message. Before drafting, name the reader's
present task, what the offering actually lets them do, the evidence that supports it,
and the immediate next action. An internal process is useful only when its relevance to
that decision is legible. Do not make the visitor infer a benefit from a role count,
method name, evidence ledger or abstract statement of rigor.

CXL's homepage guidance asks a value proposition to establish what the offering is,
what the visitor can do, its usefulness and its relevant distinction. Category clarity
and useful specificity precede cleverness. This is practitioner guidance, not a promised
conversion effect for another website. [CXL](https://cxl.com/blog/saas-homepage-optimization/)

For this run, distinguish:

- **Verified capability:** what the product demonstrably does or its authoritative source
  documents. Preserve the difference between documented capability and observed outcome.
- **User value:** why that capability matters for the named task. Keep the inference no
  stronger than its evidence; an available review is not guaranteed beautiful output.
- **Proof:** the example, behavior, source or concrete mechanism supporting that statement.
  A conceptual diagram is explanation, not an observed customer result.
- **Action:** what this page makes possible immediately. A guide link is not installation,
  and copying a command is not running it.

## Choose a formula for a job, not a page for a formula

The following are writing heuristics. A recognizable acronym is neither evidence nor a
quality score. Select only the methods that resolve a named communication problem. Do not
concatenate all of them, require an equal section for each letter, or ship their names as
headings. The fit and rejection conditions below are OMD's application limits.

| Method | Useful question / application | Do not force it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AIDA: attention, interest, desire, action | Does the opening establish relevance, a reason to continue and a credible reason to act? | The reader already knows the offer and needs a direct instruction. |
| PAS: problem, agitation, solution | Does a real, evidenced difficulty make the solution's relevance concrete? | The problem, emotion or cost would have to be invented. Never manufacture anxiety for the agitation step. |
| 4P: problem, promise, proof, proposal | Can a skeptical reader connect the offered change to evidence and a clear next step? | The promise or proof is unavailable. Reduce the claim; do not fabricate a testimonial or guarantee. |

These three structures are summarized from Joanna Wiebe's practitioner catalogue, which
also distinguishes page, headline and action formulas. Their order is a drafting aid, not
an OMD workflow claim. [Copyhackers](https://copyhackers.com/2015/10/copywriting-formula/)

| Method | Useful question / application | Do not force it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FAB: feature, advantage, benefit | Translate a supported feature into its practical consequence for this reader. It is an analysis chain, not three repetitive shipped sentences. | The claimed advantage requires an untested comparison, saving or quality uplift. |
| BAB: before, after, bridge | Explain a supported change and the mechanism connecting the two states. | There is no real comparison. Do not present an imagined after-state as an achieved result. |
| 4C: clear, concise, compelling, credible | Check whether each line is understandable, earns its length, matters to this reader and is supportable. | Brevity would erase a necessary condition, accurate category or recovery instruction. |

Buffer presents these as drafting and editing devices, primarily for social content;
transferring them to a website is an OMD hypothesis, not evidence of website conversion.
[Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/copywriting-formulas/)

| Method | Useful question / application | Do not force it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4U headline check: useful, unique, ultra-specific, urgent | Test usefulness and concrete distinction. Include urgency only when an actual deadline or consequence supports it. | It would create fake scarcity, an unverified superlative or arbitrary numerical precision. |

Copyblogger describes the 4U approach for headlines and connects the hook to the opening
paragraph. OMD does not turn every U into a mandatory claim.
[Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/write-like-a-hurricane/)

## Turn research into actual copy

1. **Establish the input, not a fictional persona.** Use the current brief, verified fact
   ledger and cited audience evidence. Record assumed awareness or motivation as an
   assumption, not a customer quotation or tested preference. Public category discussion
   may supply language; it cannot prove this product's performance.
2. **Write the plain offer before the slogan.** In ordinary target-language prose, explain
   what the reader can do with this offering and why the named mechanism matters. If that
   sentence cannot be supported, return the factual gap instead of styling around it.
3. **Test genuinely different opening angles.** For a rejected or unsettled marketing
   opening, draft a small set of complete headline/support/action combinations from the
   same facts: for example, a useful capability, an evidenced problem, or a concrete
   working distinction. Record the count and why these angles differ before drafting.
   Word substitutions of one slogan are not alternatives. No angle inherits approval
   from the old copy, art direction, font proof or a formula's reputation.
4. **Choose the message sequence.** Each region answers a new reader question. Put its
   answer in the heading or first sentence, connect claims to nearby proof, and place
   prerequisites and installation detail at the action they affect. Required content
   survives, but README order and exhaustive internal taxonomy are not a landing outline.
5. **Translate features into relevance.** A method count or process verb alone is not a
   benefit. Explain the supported consequence once; keep the mechanism where it makes
   that consequence believable. Do not replace a specific capability with an empty
   aspiration such as better results, effortless work or next-level quality.
6. **Edit as Korean, not translated marketing English.** Preserve one suitable register,
   restore actor/action/condition where needed, and use concrete task vocabulary. Do not
   ban nouns, questions, contrasts or technical terms wholesale. A short noun label may
   be exactly right; a technically correct paragraph can still obscure the offer.
7. **Predict the click.** The action label and adjacent support identify the immediate
   destination or state. Simplifying the wording must not conceal setup, payment,
   prerequisites or a later actor. Keep exact commands and truthful feedback intact.

The web-reading studies by Morkes and Nielsen support concise, scannable, factual text in
their tested settings. Use informative entry points and one useful idea per paragraph;
do not transplant historical percentages into a forecast for this page.
[NN/G](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/)

Toss's eight principles distinguish unnecessary words, duplicate sentences and unnecessary
information between screens. They also emphasize understandable next-screen hints and
spoken clarity. Borrow those communication tests, not Toss's identity or a default Korean
speech level. [Toss UX writing](https://toss.tech/article/21022)

Toss's marketing article reports tests of its own in-app banners and recommends one main
message and concrete actions/conditions. Its reported click and exposure differences are
not controlled evidence for this landing, and its reward examples do not authorize a new
offer here. [Toss marketing writing](https://toss.tech/article/Marketing_Writing)

Mailchimp prioritizes clarity and distinguishes stable voice from situation-dependent tone.
That supports deliberate register, not imitation of its humor or a universal friendly voice.
[Mailchimp](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/)

W3C recommends descriptive headings, meaningful link text and clear instructions. These
apply to visible text and its accessible semantics; a catchy CTA must still communicate
its destination. [W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tips/writing/)

## Record application, not a checklist of names

Inside the existing **Humanize audit**, add a compact **Marketing message map** when this
pack applies: region; reader question; actual answer; verified fact IDs; selected method
and the wording/order change it caused. Record the opening angles, selected choice and
rejected alternatives in the writer's existing decision entry. These are analytical
records, not user-facing copy. No new standalone artifact or self-awarded quality score.

Applied means an actual line or information sequence changed for a stated reason. Merely
listing AIDA, citing an article, adding an adjective or repeating a feature under a
benefit label does not establish application. Preserve the factual ledger and exact
functional labels; return an inaccurate frozen label to the framer for correction.

## Independent copy review

The copy editor first reads only the proposed surface strings in their intended reading
order. Before considering the writer's audit or formula rationale, state in its own words:
what is offered, for whom, what task it helps with, why that is credible, and what the
primary action does. Then verify those answers against the allowed facts and voice.
This is a model-based comprehension proxy, not a timed human study or conversion test.

Reject a marketing draft when the offer only becomes understandable in the audit; when
methods are listed without relevant user value; when every heading could fit an unrelated
offering; when the hook is not fulfilled; or when the formula creates repetition or an
unsupported promise. A clear factual category, specific technical mechanism, necessary
qualification or direct instruction is not a defect merely for being unpoetic.

Return exact lines and their reader consequence through the existing copy-editor report.
The writer repairs the deck. Changed copy invalidates affected copy review, typography,
composition and rendered evidence; previous approval never blesses the new words. Verify
the current deck and actual rendered line lengths after the revision. Human preference,
comprehension or conversion improvement requires independent target-reader evidence.
