# Design language translation v1

Preserve the complete user request. After domain analysis, the coordinator may propose a `design-language-input-v1` reading of casual Korean or English feedback. The lexicon in `core/theory/design-language.lexicon.v1.json` supplies contextual hypotheses, not universal taste laws. A model-proposed reading uses the same registered metrics. `omd language translate --input ...` publishes an exact source-contract-bound, content-addressed interpretation; `omd language check` reads it. Never use adjectives alone as a prescription or keywords alone as evidence.

The design lane searches English names for parts and mechanisms while the domain lane retains local market/service qualification. A pending interpretation may drive search, not production. When two materially distinct readings survive, ask one plain-Korean question showing two or three retained design-reference thumbnails with admitted native acquisition and image digests. An arbitrary local PNG, a search result or an unsigned gallery wrapper cannot back a choice. Do not fabricate thumbnails or ask for routine design approval. A selected reading projects only measured targets, destinations, and its digest to art direction, composition and Hand; raw feedback, brand identity and source pixels remain with the coordinator and Scout. Never copy named-product branding or let Hand rewrite approved copy. Keep a white canvas with distinct primary, secondary and accent roles as the default product reading; neither beige/editorial treatment nor 60-30-10 percentages are hard rules.

Feedback translation is not authorization to change application source. First measure the current native render at the same route, state and viewport; do not accept caller-authored baseline numbers. Bounded Hand repair follows existing write authority, then fresh native evidence, a passing movement check and existing independent refinement review. `omd feedback check` compares the native before/after scoped measurements and refuses no movement, lost destinations, and local scope-floor failures. It does not claim independent review or final task/accessibility/safety coverage: refinement checkpoint and completion revalidate the current signed production observations and Eye review. A better Eye preference cannot override unmet movement. A translation that rejects overall direction reopens art direction or composition; copy-tone readings instead reopen Writer and require a changed deck plus current CLEAN copy review. Copy-tone targets never appear in Hand's visual projection, and the copy-stage skip remains a skip rather than selecting a new stage. Selected route skips remain skips.

Executable refusal codes: `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_INVALID`, `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_REQUIRED`, `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_UNGROUNDED`, `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_AMBIGUOUS`, `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_STALE`, `DESIGN_LANGUAGE_BINDING_MISSING`, `FEEDBACK_MEASUREMENT_REQUIRED`, `FEEDBACK_BASELINE_STALE`, `FEEDBACK_TARGET_UNMET`. Refused publication must not alter application source or current pointers. Advisory seed intervals are contextual, not universal aesthetic thresholds. `AI-looking` prompts inspection of concrete slop findings, never a claim about authorship.
