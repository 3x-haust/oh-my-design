# Design practice

OMD's motto is **AI that designs like a human**. Its operational meaning is to make and test
design judgments against people, tasks, material, and rendered outcomes. A long process, a polished
rationale, or a large number of artifacts is not evidence of a good design.

This source-free contract applies within the selected route and existing ownership boundaries.
It adds no universal stage, research quota, approval checkpoint, or artifact family. The specialized
protocols retain their schemas, publishers, isolation, and final gates. Record only bounded decisions
and observations that another person can inspect, never hidden reasoning.

## Choose the next useful test

Start from the consequential uncertainty that could change the design. Distinguish a problem about
people's needs from one about content, structure, visual expression, interaction, or implementation.
Select the least elaborate representation that can answer it: a source read, conversation evidence,
content outline, parallel sketch, real-copy type specimen, visual draft, or working interaction.
Use production fidelity when the question depends on real layout, state, motion, or input behavior.
A low-fidelity prototype cannot settle a high-fidelity question.

Treat a hypothesis as consequential when its outcome could change a required task, a costly error,
or a major content-to-form decision. Name in the existing owner artifact:

- The claim or decision ID, its task/context, and the evidence that is missing.
- The predicted observation and the observation that would contradict it, before testing.
- The selected test, actual evidence reference, observed result, and remaining uncertainty.
- The resulting choice: retain, revise locally, or return the assumption to its upstream owner.

Use the frame narrative for framing questions, decision-graph entries for actual alternatives,
craft records for in-build judgments, and the selected browser/repair records for real outcomes.
Do not add fields to a closed schema. `hypothesis-validation` in the route selects the hypothesis
recording procedure above; the other sections apply to their selected work independently. The method
name alone does not establish that a hypothesis was tested. An untested claim remains a
hypothesis, and an inconclusive result remains unresolved. Neither becomes a confirmed user fact.

Plan around observed uncertainty and error cost. A repeated failed query or transport retry adds
no design knowledge: diagnose its cause, choose another authorized source or test, or report the
specific gap. Reuse current evidence when it answers the question. Stop gathering at coverage.

## Frame from people and material

Framer separates supplied facts, observations of real people, external research, inferred needs,
and temporary decisions. A designer's story, competitor interface, imagined persona, or AI role-play
can suggest a question; none establishes what this product's users do. Never invent interviews,
quotes, participant counts, demand, or preferences. If participant access is unavailable, preserve
that limit and use a reversible hypothesis where the task permits it.

Describe the person's situation, intended outcome, work object, constraints, and costly failure
before proposing the interface. Look at current workarounds and what happens before and after the
screen when evidence is available. Preserve explicit user scope while challenging an inferred
solution. A new frame needs evidence or user authority, not permission to invent capabilities.

Use the real content and representative difficult cases: long titles, sparse and dense records,
mixed scripts, uncertain results, and applicable errors. Do not invent a state just to fill a test
matrix. Copy and data are design material; their length, meaning, relationships, and priority should
shape the layout. Return an upstream copy or fact mismatch to its owner.

## Explore without fixation

When the route selects alternatives, state what consequential relationship each tests before
making it: what dominates, what is adjacent, how the reading or work path moves, and how the task
fits on mobile. Colour or margin changes alone do not constitute structural alternatives.
Keep candidate construction independent until the selected comparison; do not let an early polished
example become every candidate's skeleton. Follow the concept protocol's evidence-based count.

Study references for the named decision, including a conflicting example when it can resolve an
uncertainty. An example supplies an observable mechanism and its conditions, not an instruction to
copy its whole page. Composer translates only the allowed sanitized evidence into the destination's
content-to-form relationship. Raw references and source identities never enter a blind review.

After comparison, useful principles from a rejected alternative may inform a new owner-authored
candidate. This is a new, re-rendered hypothesis, not permission to average layouts or silently alter
the selected design. Respect the existing bounded recovery and publication contracts.

## Reflect while making

At each selected craft checkpoint, compare the real render with its named criterion. Separate the
visible observation from the judgment and the resulting action. Choose `revise` for a concrete
defect, `retain` when the evidence supports the existing choice, or `reframe` when the premise is
wrong. Follow the craft CLI contract in the human design loop; retention requires evidence, not
"looks fine". Do not change a good design just to populate a modification field.

A revised artifact needs fresh observation. A retained artifact still faces the existing final
checks and independent review. Reframing returns the contradicted premise to its owner and pauses
dependent work until the required contract is current. A craft reframe note does not itself mutate
the route, reopen a stage, or authorize a second production owner. On source-only production hosts,
request the authorized observation through the owner handoff; never claim to have inspected an
unavailable render or fabricate a checkpoint from source inspection.

Treat a failed approach as useful evidence when it actually happened. Never fabricate failure or
compromise to fill a trade-off record. A selected contract whose required evidence is unavailable
remains incomplete; a prose judgment cannot waive its gate.

## Critique and learn

Critique asks whether the supplied result serves the person, task, and selected criteria. Name the
visible defect or observed behavior, its consequence, and the smallest test that would resolve the
objection. Do not reward effort, trendiness, author explanations, or a change merely for being new.
When observations conflict, retain the disagreement and test the disputed premise; majority opinion
alone does not resolve it. Preserve reviewer judgments exactly and reconcile adopted decisions
through the owner, not by editing a review to agree.

Keep evidence classes separate. A browser action proves its scripted outcome. Automated checks
cover their particular accessibility rules. An isolated AI critique is a visual-quality proxy.
Real participants performing relevant tasks supply usability evidence, and named-audience human
ratings are needed for human cultural-fit claims. One class cannot impersonate the others.

After a tested outcome, retain the decision, applicable context, evidence, contradiction, and next
question through the existing learning contract. Reuse a lesson only inside its supported scope;
neither one successful page nor repeated agent agreement establishes a universal design rule.
Completion means current required outcomes and review gates hold. A plateau with unmet criteria
remains incomplete; extra iterations and manufactured changes do not make it complete.
