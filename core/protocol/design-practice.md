# Design practice

OMD's motto is **AI that designs like a human**. Its operational meaning is to make and test
design judgments against people, tasks, material, and rendered outcomes. A long process, a polished
rationale, or a large number of artifacts is not evidence of a good design.

This method contract applies inside the single model-led route and its write boundaries. It adds no
extra stage, role relay, research quota, approval checkpoint, or artifact family. The implementing
model chooses useful tests; selected evidence contracts retain their schemas, publishers, isolation,
and final gates. Record only bounded decisions and observations that another person can inspect,
never hidden reasoning.

## Choose the next useful test

Start from the consequential uncertainty that could change the design. Distinguish a problem about
people's needs from one about content, structure, visual expression, interaction, or implementation.
Select the least elaborate representation that can answer it: a source read, conversation evidence,
content outline, parallel sketch, real-copy type specimen, visual draft, or working interaction.
Use production fidelity when the question depends on real layout, state, motion, or input behavior.
A low-fidelity prototype cannot settle a high-fidelity question.

Treat a hypothesis as consequential when its outcome could change a required task, a costly error,
or a major content-to-form decision. Name in the existing owner artifact:

- The claim or decision ID, its task/context, and the evidence that is missing.
- The predicted observation and the observation that would contradict it, before testing.
- The selected test, actual evidence reference, observed result, and remaining uncertainty.
- The resulting choice: retain, revise locally, or return the assumption to its upstream owner.

Use the frame narrative for framing questions, decision-graph entries for actual alternatives,
craft records for in-build judgments, and the selected browser/repair records for real outcomes.
Do not add fields to a closed schema. `hypothesis-validation` in the route selects the hypothesis
recording procedure above; the other sections apply to their selected work independently. The method
name alone does not establish that a hypothesis was tested. An untested claim remains a
hypothesis, and an inconclusive result remains unresolved. Neither becomes a confirmed user fact.

Plan around observed uncertainty and error cost. A repeated failed query or transport retry adds
no design knowledge: diagnose its cause, choose another authorized source or test, or report the
specific gap. Reuse current evidence when it answers the question. Stop gathering at coverage.

## Frame from people and material

Framer separates supplied facts, observations of real people, external research, inferred needs,
and temporary decisions. A designer's story, competitor interface, imagined persona, or AI role-play
can suggest a question; none establishes what this product's users do. Never invent interviews,
quotes, participant counts, demand, or preferences. If participant access is unavailable, preserve
that limit and use a reversible hypothesis where the task permits it.

Describe the person's situation, intended outcome, work object, constraints, and costly failure
before proposing the interface. Look at current workarounds and what happens before and after the
screen when evidence is available. Preserve explicit user scope while challenging an inferred
solution. A new frame needs evidence or user authority, not permission to invent capabilities.

Use the real content and representative difficult cases: long titles, sparse and dense records,
mixed scripts, uncertain results, and applicable errors. Do not invent a state just to fill a test
matrix. Copy and data are design material; their length, meaning, relationships, and priority should
shape the layout. Return an upstream copy or fact mismatch to its owner.

When the requested meaning spans the whole page, carry its complete question/content/action
sequence through the existing Frame surfaces, Composer expansion and current captures. A
polished entry viewport or isolated component does not prove the remaining sequence. When
video, a loop or a time-based demonstration is itself meaningful task content, put its
complete applicable progression and completion/recovery condition in the existing required
outcomes and browser evidence. A first frame or short motion filmstrip proves only its bounded
observed interval, not the whole medium. The protocol reviewer must judge the actual scope
against those observations and return insufficient when coverage is missing. Ordinary static
work gains no video or whole-page testing quota. A generated demo is explanatory material,
not verified product performance, customer evidence or permission to invent a claim.

## Explore without fixation

When the route selects alternatives, state what consequential relationship each tests before
making it: what dominates, what is adjacent, how the reading or work path moves, and how the task
fits on mobile. Colour or margin changes alone do not constitute structural alternatives.
Keep candidate construction independent until the selected comparison; do not let an early polished
example become every candidate's skeleton. Art Director commits a minimal seed and 2-3 hypotheses
for one representative surface before Sketch source, using identical real content/state/views.
Interactive users choose once from actual previews; only explicit autonomous authority permits blind
Eye choice. Typesetter and Composer develop the chosen relationship afterward, before full expansion.

Study references for the named decision, including a conflicting example when it can resolve an
uncertainty. An example supplies an observable mechanism and its conditions, not an instruction to
copy its whole page. Current makers inspect selected rights-allowed whole-screen references with
actual Scout analysis, then translate the relationship into the destination's own content and tokens.
Preserve external originals whole and keep the existing 2x screenshot contract. Describe a region
of interest on the whole image; a pixel crop must not replace that evidence or its task context.
Reference images, source identities and maker
rationale never enter blind review.

After comparison, useful principles from a rejected alternative may inform a new owner-authored
candidate. This is a new, re-rendered hypothesis, not permission to average layouts or silently alter
the selected design. Respect the existing bounded recovery and publication contracts.

## Reflect while making

For newly routed candidate work, visual fidelity is not a showpiece privilege. Compare integrated
representative surfaces with real copy, approved typography and colour, and responsive composition.
A restrained product keeps its quiet expression without postponing visual judgment. A grayscale
study can answer a structural question, but cannot establish a finished visual direction.
The coordinator's `candidate select` publisher captures the selected first-party HTML at fixed
1280x900 and 390x844 viewports. Its v2 pointer binds the complete served tree and both PNGs;
production receives those exact images as design material by default. Changed dependencies or
capture bytes invalidate the handoff. This exception concerns first-party candidate pixels, never
Scout-owned external reference pixels or a blind final review's inputs.

With a selected design hypothesis, inspect the first representative implementation before expanding
its visual system across the product. `first-render check` captures both fixed viewports. On native
Pi it automatically runs one isolated early Eye through the one-use evidence broker, inheriting the
host model with the Eye's high effort. One early critique keeps this checkpoint small; the existing
independent final review remains separate and unchanged. Other hosts inspect both saved captures
through their preproduction Eye and disclose the actual isolation available.
For source-only production transactions, settle representative visual questions in the candidate
stage first; the initial source owner still completes its authorized scope in one transaction.
The coordinator then captures production and routes corrections through the existing repair owner.

The early Eye returns `retain`, `revise`, or `reframe` with localized visible observations, user
consequences, and testable repair criteria. Numeric diagnostics stay advisory. `revise` returns to
the authorized production owner for repair, rebuild and recapture; `reframe` returns the premise to
Composer. A fresh review must explicitly resolve or preserve each prior criterion. Identical
capture, build and brief bindings reuse the previous review, including an unfavorable one.
Each unresolved criterion keeps the authenticated production captures from the review that first
introduced it. Repeated repair and candidate reselection do not move this baseline. The early Eye
receives those exact prior pixels separately from the current production and current selected target.
It judges absolute current quality, target fidelity, and repair progress separately; missing,
changed, or misbound baseline evidence refuses review publication without replacing the current record.
The signed early record is not final evidence, task completion, or permission to bypass source
ownership. Its initial viewport proves only that bounded surface, not unbuilt downstream screens.

At each selected craft checkpoint, compare the real render with its named criterion. Separate the
visible observation from the judgment and the resulting action. Choose `revise` for a concrete
defect, `retain` when the evidence supports the existing choice, or `reframe` when the premise is
wrong. Follow the craft CLI contract in the human design loop; retention requires evidence, not
"looks fine". Do not change a good design just to populate a modification field.

A revised artifact needs fresh observation. A retained artifact still faces the existing final
checks and independent review. Reframing returns the contradicted premise to its owner and pauses
dependent work until the required contract is current. A craft reframe note does not itself mutate
the route, reopen a stage, or authorize a second production owner. On source-only production hosts,
request the authorized observation through the owner handoff; never claim to have inspected an
unavailable render or fabricate a checkpoint from source inspection.

At the native candidate fidelity boundary, refinement of the same candidate carries its
authenticated prior assessment and repair criteria under the same build, brief and reference
context. The reviewer binds each prior and current slot/viewport capture and classifies the
relationship as preserved, resolved, regressed or unresolved. These are continuity judgments,
not a summed beauty score. A formerly passing counterpart can fail on the new pixels; every
current counterpart must still pass before selection. A changed reference context starts a
fresh comparison rather than laundering earlier criteria into new authority. Unchanged rejected
pixels retain their unfavorable result instead of receiving repeated approval attempts.

Treat a failed approach as useful evidence when it actually happened. Never fabricate failure or
compromise to fill a trade-off record. A selected contract whose required evidence is unavailable
remains incomplete; a prose judgment cannot waive its gate.

## Optional repair techniques

Use these while making when they answer a real uncertainty, not as another required
document, concept count, approval step or sequence. The ordinary model-led author may
work directly; selected source-owner and evidence contracts remain unchanged.

Start with the competing content relationship: which information explains, qualifies,
compares with or acts on which other information? Distinguish a visible condition from
its suspected cause. Preserve observed strengths, identify the actual defect, and keep
unknown task priorities explicit. Choose a local correction when the structure works;
reconstruct the relationship when styling alone would preserve the problem.

- For a weak price comparison, compose one complete plan with its real name, price,
  billing unit, qualification, differences and action. Put contrasting plans beside it
  before repeating the pattern; check long names and unequal content. Do not conceal
  renewal terms, flatten meaningful differences or treat one attractive card as proof
  of the complete comparison or its mobile behavior.
- For a busy detail sheet, preserve identity, valid actions and recovery while testing
  whether boundaries communicate real groups, independent states or false clickability.
  Removing a background can break grouping as easily as restore it. Recheck short and
  long content, the scrolled heading, close path and action reachability where applicable.
- If a photographic composition still obstructs reading after a real adjustment, test
  a different image/text placement or material. Abandon a failed intermediate treatment
  instead of escalating overlays, shadows or decorative fixes because time was invested.
  There is no duty to manufacture a failed experiment when a direct repair works.

Inspect the whole current render after propagation, not just the repaired unit. Keep
actual content, requested states, source identity and explicit destination constraints
in view. Containers, ornaments, density and whitespace are means, not winning recipes.
Existing independent review judges the new pixels; a technique or favorable expert
opinion does not prove absolute beauty, usability, conversion or human satisfaction.

## Critique and learn

Critique asks whether the supplied result serves the person, task, and selected criteria. Name the
visible defect or observed behavior, its consequence, and the smallest test that would resolve the
objection. Do not reward effort, trendiness, author explanations, or a change merely for being new.
When observations conflict, retain the disagreement and test the disputed premise; majority opinion
alone does not resolve it. Preserve reviewer judgments exactly and reconcile adopted decisions
through the owner, not by editing a review to agree.

Keep evidence classes separate. A browser action proves its scripted outcome. Automated checks
cover their particular accessibility rules. An isolated AI critique is a visual-quality proxy.
Real participants performing relevant tasks supply usability evidence, and named-audience human
ratings are needed for human cultural-fit claims. One class cannot impersonate the others.

After a tested outcome, retain the decision, applicable context, evidence, contradiction, and next
question through the existing learning contract. Reuse a lesson only inside its supported scope;
neither one successful page nor repeated agent agreement establishes a universal design rule.
Completion means current required outcomes and review gates hold. A plateau with unmet criteria
remains incomplete; extra iterations and manufactured changes do not make it complete.
