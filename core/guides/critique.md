# Critique

Review only; do not patch unless implementation is separately requested. Inspect current
fixed-view renders across the requested surfaces and states plus actual native measurements.
Missing pixels or numbers stay unassessed. A screenshot does not prove accessible names,
keyboard tasks or screen-reader behavior.

A same-session critique is useful but is not independent approval; independent review runs
inside `omd first-render check` and `omd lifecycle finish`.

Group findings by actual cause and rank by user consequence. Cite localized conditions and
measurement IDs rather than generic taste. Familiar controls or restrained typography are not
defects by themselves. Separate required defects from optional polish; do not invent problems.
