# Writing and repairing copy

Identify speaker, listener, situation, intended change, register and the exact verified facts.
Missing consequential facts are withheld, not invented.

Repair locally when the order works; rebuild from the same facts when the discourse is broken.
Put the answer or action first, keep actor/action/condition concrete, place qualifiers beside
their claims and remove repetition that adds no decision. Preserve useful labels, accessible
repetition, legal limits and natural register. Do not add fake warmth, quirks or a formulaic
title-plus-explanatory-subtitle under every heading.

Compare every proposition with the facts. Preserve numbers, names, uncertainty and quotations.
UI action and status text must describe what actually happens (for example a demo does not
"send" anything). Korean variable input is data, not a sentence stem with guessed particles.
Changed visible copy needs fresh renders.
